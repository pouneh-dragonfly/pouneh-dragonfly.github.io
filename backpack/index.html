Investment Report · Backpack (BP)

Tokenised Stocks, Done Properly

A global digital brokerage for stocks and digital assets, and a token that can turn into equity at IPO or sale

Backpack has done the slow, expensive thing most of those racing to put stocks on blockchain rails have skipped: it got licensed country by country, then used those licences to put genuine, redeemable US shares on a 24/7 onchain market. Within four months it was handling roughly three quarters of onchain trading in the shares it issues. The BP token gives holders no dividend, but it does carry something no comparable token offers: a legal right to swap into Backpack equity when the company lists or is sold.

What Backpack Actually Does

What it is
An online broker, in the same family as Interactive Brokers or Robinhood, that also runs its own trading market and settles trades on a blockchain instead of through traditional back-office systems.
What a customer can do
Open one account, then buy US shares and ETFs, trade digital assets like Bitcoin, lend out idle cash for interest, borrow against holdings and move money in dollars, euros or yen.
What makes it different
Any share bought through Backpack can be turned into a digital token that trades 24/7, including weekends, and converted back into an ordinary share that can be moved to Schwab or Fidelity at any time.
How it makes money
Mainly trading fees, plus interest on lending, like any broker.
Where it operates
Licensed in Dubai and the EU, with a US broker-dealer for its stock business and an application pending in Japan. Roughly half the world's population can use it today.
01 · The Plain-English Version

Think Of It As An ETF Creation Desk For Every Stock

The mechanism that keeps an ETF honest, applied to single shares that trade around the clock

Every professional investor knows why an ETF trades close to the value of what it holds. Authorised participants can hand in the underlying shares and receive new ETF units, or do the reverse, so any gap between the two prices is quickly arbitraged away. The price stays honest because the wrapper can always be swapped back for the real thing.

Backpack Securities applies that same plumbing to individual US stocks on a blockchain. When someone buys Backpack's SpaceX token on Solana, a real share is bought and held in custody at a US brokerage. The token cannot exist without the share behind it. Holders can move it into their Schwab or Fidelity account the way they would transfer holdings between brokers, or bring stock the other way and trade them on a Sunday afternoon.

Most rival products do not work like this. Robinhood's and Kraken's stock tokens are separate debt securities that track a share price, closer to a contract for difference than to owning the stock. Because they cannot be swapped for the underlying share, traders struggle to arbitrage them back into line, and that shows up directly in the price customers pay.

In the early rush to trade stocks around the clock, few buyers are asking what actually stands behind the token. We think they soon will. As the market matures and attracts more serious money, real share-for-share backing will become a deciding factor in where customers trade, and a major driver of growth.

Today's market is like having to visit a guarded building, sign in and show ID just to get a glass of water. Tokenisation puts a tap in every home, open to anyone, anywhere.Armani Ferrante, Backpack CEO, paraphrased from the Frictionless Capital podcast, September 2026

Around that stock product sits a full trading platform for digital assets: spot and futures trading, a lending pool, a self-custody wallet, and bank transfers in dollars, euros and yen. Everything sits in one account, so a customer can borrow against their SpaceX stock and spend the dollars, which is prime brokerage for retail investors.

The company was founded in 2022 by Armani Ferrante, the engineer behind Anchor, the toolkit most Solana software is built with. It lost $14.5m, roughly 88% of its cash, when FTX collapsed that November, survived by launching the Mad Lads NFT collection, and has spent the three years since collecting licences rather than chasing volume. It has raised $37m, all as equity and never through token sales, and its leadership includes Mark Wetjen, a former acting chairman of the US derivatives regulator, the CFTC.

Why Solana. Ferrante's logic was simple: if blockchains were going to run real finance, they would need to be fast and cheap, and in 2022 Solana was the only network that could handle that load. Loyalty matters too. When FTX collapsed and Solana was widely written off as dead, its co-founders Anatoly Yakovenko and Raj Gokal personally phoned teams across the ecosystem to check they were still standing. Backpack stayed, and today Ferrante calls Solana the only decentralised network operating at genuine scale.

02 · Traction And Adoption

Fees, Users And A Dominant Share Of A New Market

A two-engine business: a mature digital-asset trading business paying the bills, and a stock product growing fast

Fees and revenue

Backpack is reported to have earned more than $100m of revenue in 2025, almost entirely from trading fees on Crypto spot and perpetual futures. That figure comes from press reports around its early-2026 fundraising rather than audited accounts, so we treat it as indicative. Ferrante adds a striking counterpoint: by refusing to serve customers in countries where it lacked a licence, he estimates Backpack gave up $100m to $200m of profit last year. Few founders volunteer that kind of cost.

Cumulative trading volume tells the growth story. The exchange had handled $27.5bn during its beta in March 2024, $219bn by November 2025, and more than $400bn by mid-2026.

Users

The exchange reached about 100,000 monthly active users by mid-2026, on top of roughly 475,000 people who took part in its pre-launch points programme and 110,000 former FTX Europe customers it inherited. The stock product has added a new audience quickly. Backpack's SpaceX token passed 10,000 holders in six days, nearly double its nearest rival, and on 6 October the count of wallets holding Backpack-issued stocks crossed 300,000.

Market share

The headline number is the gap between supply and trading. Backpack issues only about 5% of the tokenised stock supply on Solana, yet its tokens captured around 73% of trading volume in July, $1.06bn in a single month. In the second quarter it took 87% of all onchain SpaceX trading and 95% of Micron. Solana, in turn, hosted 82% of global tokenised equity trading in July. Backpack is therefore the top issuer on the leading network, after just four months.

Backpack's own tally makes the pace plain: from a single stock in June to 73 tokenised assets in four months, and more than $5bn of cumulative trading. The curve steepened noticeably after the SEC's mid-September exemption, which suggests demand is accelerating rather than fading after the SpaceX launch excitement.

That last chart is the ETF analogy in numbers. Because Backpack's tokens can be redeemed for real shares, professional traders keep them priced within a whisker of Nasdaq. Customers paid roughly one twenty-seventh of the cost on mid-sized trades and about one forty-fifth on small ones, compared with Robinhood's chain.

03 · Competitive Advantages

Four Edges, And How Long Each Should Last

The licences are the hardest to copy; the stock structure is already being copied
Regulatory

One licensed exchange, many countries

Dubai (VARA, 2023), the EU through the former FTX Europe MiFID II licence and a Latvian MiCA and payments licence granted in May 2026, a US broker-dealer for the stock business, and a multi-year application in Japan. The aim is one global order book, like a bank with branches rather than a franchise of separate local banks. Binance, with vastly more money, has just withdrawn from Europe.

Durability: high. Years of work per jurisdiction, and impossible to shortcut.
Product

Real, redeemable shares

The ETF-style redemption route is why its tokens trade tightly and why traders concentrate there. Deep markets then attract more traders, the classic exchange network effect.

Durability: medium. Securitize launched the same structure on Solana on 8 October with Jump Trading as market maker.
Trust

Proof of reserves every single day

The exchange is built like a blockchain: every order and fill is signed and replayable, and customer balances are proven daily with a zero-knowledge proof. Most exchanges do this quarterly at best. For a firm with FTX in its history, this is the equivalent of publishing a daily audited NAV.

Durability: high. Hard to retrofit onto older exchange systems.
Platform

Everything in one margin account

Crypto, stocks, lending, futures and fiat sit in one portfolio, so shares can be collateral and idle cash earns lending yield. Prime brokerage for the retail investor.

Durability: medium. Incumbents can build this, but slowly across licensed entities.

Ferrante calls the strategy a "last-mover advantage": learning from every predecessor's compliance mistakes and building without that legacy. The honest read is that the licence stack is a genuine moat, while the stock structure is a head start. Within a week, Securitize has launched a matching product and OKX with Intercontinental Exchange, the owner of the NYSE, has filed to run a 24/7 tokenised stock venue. Backpack's lead now rests on keeping its liquidity and execution advantage while bigger names arrive.

04 · Growth Drivers

From 200 Stocks To 10,000, And From Half The World To All Of It

Most of Backpack's growth comes from markets it is not yet allowed to serve

Opening the doors. Backpack says it currently serves only about 48% of the world, and its core exchange is not yet available in the United States or Japan. Unlike offshore rivals, nobody can use a VPN to get round its identity checks. Each new country therefore adds genuinely new customers, and the token unlock schedule is tied directly to these launches.

Japan as a specific catalyst. Ferrante has lived in Tokyo for nearly four years working with the regulator. Japan once generated over half of global Bitcoin volume, and the government is expected to cut the tax on Crypto gains from as high as 55% to 20%. Backpack would arrive as one of very few licensed newcomers.

The whole stock market, onchain. Backpack has issued 73 tokenised assets since June and in the last week added the iShares Brazil ETF, Cerebras and BlackRock. Ferrante's stated aim is to go from dozens of tickers to 10,000, the full US market, in one step. Backpack's research partner estimates that even a thousandfold increase in today's tokenised equities would cover only about 6% of global equity value. The runway is very long.

US regulation turning helpful. On 17 September the SEC granted a five-year Innovation Exemption allowing approved venues to trade tokenised US-listed stocks onchain, and it explicitly excludes synthetic products. That favours the real-share model Backpack already runs. Bringing regulated Crypto futures to US customers would be a further step-change, and it is one of the milestones the company has named.

The exemption is narrow: each venue's onchain trading is capped at 0.25% of a stock's average daily volume. Kyle Samani, who sits on Backpack's board, noted this week that Backpack Securities' volumes already exceed that threshold, and described the business as still in "play mode". A board member is not a neutral witness, but the point stands: Backpack's offshore market is already larger than the lane US regulators have opened so far.

New product lines. Prediction markets, a payments card, non-US equities (Korea, Japan, Hong Kong), Treasury ETFs as onchain collateral, and builders using Backpack's licences as a back end for other applications, much as Hyperliquid partners with Kraken in the US.

05 · Valuation Comparatives

Cheap On One Lens, Full On The Other

BP holders do not own the revenue, so we price the token two ways

BP traded between roughly $1.00 and $1.15 today, after a broad market sell-off, against a high of $1.65 on 1 October and a low of $0.12 in May. With 250m tokens in circulation, that gives a circulating market cap of $250m to $290m. We value on circulating supply, as is our standard practice, and ignore the fully diluted figure.

Lens one: price against the business

Set against more than $100m of 2025 revenue, BP's circulating market cap is about 2.5 to 3 times sales. That is a fraction of the multiples paid for Coinbase, Robinhood or Hyperliquid. The catch is that BP holders have no claim on that revenue today: there is no buyback, no burn and no dividend. On this lens, BP looks cheap only if you believe value will eventually flow to holders.

Lens two: the token as a slice of the company

The more precise way to price BP is as a warrant on Backpack's equity. Each staked token converts into a fixed sliver of the company: 12.5% of the business spread across 625m tokens after one year, rising to 20% after four. Working backwards, a $1.00 token price implies the market is valuing Backpack at around $3.1bn if all 20% is earned, or $5bn on the one-year 12.5% alone. That compares with a reported funding round at a $1bn pre-money valuation in February. On this lens, the token already prices in roughly three times the last private valuation, or about 30 times 2025 revenue. That is not cheap: it sits above Robinhood, which has traded at roughly 15 to 25 times sales this year, and well above Coinbase. We think the premium is defensible. Because BP converts into equity, it can fairly be priced against listed brokerages rather than other Crypto tokens, and Backpack is growing far faster than either.

Respected investors have noticed. Citrini Research, best known for shorting Silicon Valley Bank months before it failed, gave BP a 5% weight in its published Crypto basket this week, the same as Chainlink and more than Uniswap and Ondo. All three are established Crypto names, many times BP's size, that have traded for years; BP is barely six months old. Austin Federa, formerly head of strategy at the Solana Foundation, called Backpack a top-tier team that "doesn't play the games most exchanges play". Attention of this kind helps explain the September re-rating.

Our reading: the token is priced as a growth equity, not a value trade. A $3bn implied valuation is defensible if Backpack keeps its stock market share, opens the US and Japan, and revenue compounds at the pace Hyperliquid has shown. That is our base case, and the evidence so far supports it. The key risk is that growth stalls, and with no buyback beneath it, the token would have little to cushion the fall.

06 · Tokenomics And Holder Rewards

No Insider Tokens, And A Door Into The Equity

Built so the team only gets rich if the company lists, never at launch

BP launched on 23 March 2026 with a fixed supply of one billion tokens and an unusual rule: no founder, employee or venture investor received a single token. Insiders own equity in the company instead. The supply sits in three buckets.

Ferrante's framing is Uber. The drivers built Uber's network but were never given its stock. BP is his attempt to give the people who supply Backpack's liquidity a share of what they build. Investors will recognise the structure more readily as a convertible with no coupon: you hold a tradeable token today with a right to convert into company equity at a fixed ratio when there is an exit.

What holders get today

Staking BP brings lower trading fees, down to zero for market makers at the top tier, and free international bank transfers. Backpack says further benefits are coming soon: up to 3% extra yield on dollar balances, early access to a payments card and priority allocations in future IPOs. 55% of circulating tokens are already staked, a strong signal that holders are positioning for the equity right rather than trading.

What holders may get later

After a year of continuous staking, each token converts into a fixed stake in Backpack's exchange and wallet companies, at a combined 12.5% of each across 625m tokens. Staying staked for up to three further years lifts that towards 20%. Conversion happens only at an IPO on a US exchange, a merger or an acquisition, and it is written into binding legal terms rather than a blog post.

The fine print matters

Equity arrives through non-voting shares in a holding vehicle, not directly on the company's register. The ratio is fixed against February 2026 share counts, so future fundraising dilutes it like any other shareholder. Backpack can also buy out the right at a fair value it calculates, for example if underwriters require a cleaner share register. Holders are protected by an obligation to pay fair value, but they do not control the timing.

07 · Risks

What Could Go Wrong For The Business And The Token

The biggest risks are a future supply overhang and well-funded copycats
High

The supply will more than double as the company succeeds

The 375m milestone tokens equal 150% of today's circulating supply. They go to users, not insiders, and only as new markets open, but recipients may sell. Each unlock has to create more value than the dilution it adds, which is precisely the test management has set itself.

High

The equity prize depends on an exit that may not come

No IPO date exists, and Ferrante says so plainly. Without a listing or sale, the conversion right never triggers and the treasury never unlocks. Until then the token's value rests on fee discounts and sentiment. That makes BP unusually exposed to the stock market itself: a sell-off that shuts the IPO window could hit the token harder than most Crypto assets, even if Backpack's business keeps performing.

High

Bigger rivals are copying the real-share model

Securitize launched redeemable stock tokens on Solana on 8 October backed by Jump Trading. The NYSE and the OKX and ICE venture are building 24/7 venues, and Robinhood has 27m funded customers a few taps from its own chain. Backpack's 73% share was won in an empty field.

Medium

Regulators have not named Backpack

The SEC exemption has not identified the firm as an approved venue, and listed companies can object to third-party tokenisation of their shares on those venues. The Clarity Act, which would have given broader legal certainty, has stalled in Congress.

Medium

Narrow, concentrated activity

Volume clusters in a handful of hot names such as SpaceX and Micron, and about 70% of it runs through a few professional market makers. Weekend books can be thin when the underlying market is shut.

Medium

A thin, volatile float

BP fell by half on its first day, bottomed at $0.12 in May, peaked at $1.65 on 1 October and is roughly 30% to 40% below that peak today. With 55% of circulating tokens staked, only about 112m BP, worth little more than $110m, is freely tradeable. A small float amplifies moves in both directions and limits how quickly a fund can build or exit a position.

Medium

Limited financial disclosure

Revenue and volume are self-reported and unaudited. As a private company, Backpack publishes nothing like a listed exchange's quarterly accounts.

Low

Operational stress and history

During the record October 2025 crash some users faced forced position reductions and delayed deposits. Backpack compensated affected users and suffered no losses shared across customers, but the episode, and its founders' FTX-era past, will be scrutinised in any listing. Some users also complained that the token airdrop was opaque after Backpack removed over 50m points it judged fake.

Box four needs a careful answer. Backpack has deliberately not built a buyback, which Ferrante calls a workaround. It offers the real thing instead: a contractual path into the equity that owns all of the revenue. That is arguably stronger than a buyback over a long horizon, but it pays nothing until an exit. We therefore score it closer to Collector Crypt than to the four-box winners Hyperliquid, Pump.fun, Venice and Ethena.

On fit, Backpack sits squarely inside our view that applications, not base-layer blockchains, are Crypto's new money machines, and that Solana has won the high-performance lane. It is an application with a real business that compounds Solana's lead in tokenised stocks. It is also a direct bet on our tokenisation thesis: that stablecoins exported the dollar, and tokenised shares will export the US stock market. It also brings something rare to the portfolio: a token whose value traditional investors can already recognise, because it is backed by a route into company equity.

Our mandate is long-only, unlevered and liquid, and we size higher-risk names below average. BP fits that discipline as a smaller, conviction-led position, with liquidity and the unlock schedule as the levers to watch. We would add on evidence of a US or Japan launch, audited revenue, or held market share against Securitize; we would trim if the first milestone unlock arrives without matching growth.

09 · Conclusion

A Licensed Bridge Between Two Markets, Priced As One

Backpack is the rare Crypto company that chose to be a regulated financial institution first and a token second. That choice is now paying off: when the SEC opened a lane for onchain stocks, Backpack already had the brokerage, the custody, the licences and the liquidity in place, and it took three quarters of a brand-new market within four months.

The token is equally unusual. It is not a claim on fees, it is an option on the company's eventual listing, with insiders locked out until that day. At around $1, the market already prices Backpack at roughly three times its last private round, so this is a growth bet that needs the US, Japan and the 10,000-stock roadmap to arrive on time.

BP also matters to us at the portfolio level. Much of the long-term re-rating we expect across our holdings depends on a slow shift: investors coming to treat tokens as the equal of shares, and gradually moving their activity onchain. BP does not have to wait for that shift. Its link to Backpack's equity is something every traditional investor already understands and knows how to value, which widens its potential buyer base well beyond Crypto.

Above all, we are backing the founder. Armani Ferrante saw in 2023 that Crypto and traditional finance would merge into one market, and built for it while others chased short-term volume. He kept building after losing most of his company's cash in the FTX collapse, and stayed on Solana when it was written off. That vision and staying power, the regulatory moat and the quality of the stock product justify a position. Competition is arriving quickly, so we would hold it at modest size and let execution, rather than the narrative, earn it a bigger place in the portfolio.
